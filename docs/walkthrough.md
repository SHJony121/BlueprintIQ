# Walkthrough: inspecting a synthetic drawing

All fixtures are synthetic drawings generated by `backend/scripts/generate_fixtures.py`. The rules are the demonstration thresholds in `backend/src/blueprintiq/rules/demo_rules.toml`: thickness ≥ 3 mm, diameter ≤ 50 mm, length 10–500 mm, material present. **These are not engineering standards.**

Start the server (`cd backend && uv run uvicorn blueprintiq.api.app:create_app --factory`) and use `curl` as in the README.

## 1. A drawing with violations: `violations.pdf`

Annotations: `THICKNESS: 2 mm`, `DIAMETER: 65 mm`, `LENGTH: 600 mm`, `MATERIAL: SS400`.

| Finding | Rule | Outcome | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | THK-MIN-001 | **fail** | Thickness 2 mm is below the configured minimum of 3 mm. |
| F2 | DIA-MAX-001 | **fail** | Diameter 65 mm is above the configured maximum of 50 mm. |
| F3 | LEN-RANGE-001 | **fail** | Length 600 mm is above the configured maximum of 500 mm. |
| F4 | MAT-REQ-001 | pass | A material annotation is present (SS400). This checks presence only; it does not check whether the material is suitable. |

Each finding's `evidence` holds the page and box of the text it used, so a viewer can highlight it.

## 2. Exact unit handling: `unit_conversion.pdf`

`THICKNESS: 0.3 cm` is exactly 3 mm, which meets the inclusive minimum: finding F1 is a **pass** with `actual: "3 mm (written 0.3 cm)"`. `DIAMETER: 2 in` is 50.8 mm and **fails** the 50 mm maximum. A float calculation would be at risk here; BlueprintIQ uses exact decimals.

## 3. Uncertainty is not a violation: `ambiguous.pdf`

Lines such as `THICKNESS: 4` (no unit), `DIAMETER: 2,5 mm` (2.5 or 2500?), `LENGTH: 100 ± 0.5 mm`, `THICKNESS: 4 M` (metres or a typo?) and `MATERIAL: TBD` are each kept as **ambiguous** with an issue code. Every finding is `needs_review`; none is `pass` or `fail`:

```
F1 THK-MIN-001  needs_review  not readable: M1 (missing_unit), M4 (ambiguous_unit_case)
F2 DIA-MAX-001  needs_review  not readable: M2 (decimal_comma)
```

A specification limit such as `MIN THICKNESS: 5 mm` is reported as an unrecognized annotation, never as a measurement.

## 4. Conflicts: `conflicting.pdf`

The drawing states thickness 4 mm and 6 mm, and materials SS400 and S45C. A part has one of each, so the thickness and material findings are `needs_review` ("conflicting values: 4 mm (M1); 6 mm (M2)") rather than a verdict. The two diameters (10 mm and 20 mm, two holes) are evaluated separately and both pass; the missing length is `missing`.

## 5. Missing information: `missing_material.pdf`

No material annotation: `MAT-REQ-001` is **missing** (not a failure, not a pass).

## 6. Geometry: `rotated_90.pdf`, `cropped_rotated_90.pdf`

Pages with `/Rotate` and offset crop boxes yield the same measurements; boxes are reported in page space, and the file's own crop box is returned in `document.pages[0].pdf_crop_box` for overlay mapping.

## 7. Refused files

`multi_page.pdf` → `422 TOO_MANY_PAGES`; `no_text.pdf` → `422 NO_TEXT_LAYER`; `rotate_135.pdf` → `422 UNSUPPORTED_GEOMETRY`; a non-PDF body → `415 UNSUPPORTED_FILE_TYPE`; over 10 MiB → `413 FILE_TOO_LARGE`.
